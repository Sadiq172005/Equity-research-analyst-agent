Payers / Growers / Stragglers framework: a **Grower** shows sustained double-digit revenue growth and reinvests
profit into expansion; a **Payer** has matured, modest growth, and returns cash via dividends; a **Straggler**
shows flat-to-declining revenue or profit and needs a turnaround catalyst before re-rating.

US 10-Year Treasury yield is a standard proxy for the global risk-free rate. A rising yield raises the discount
rate used on future cashflows, which tends to pressure richly-valued growth sectors (IT services, new-age tech)
more than value/dividend sectors.

Crude oil and the Indian Rupee/current account: India imports the large majority of its crude needs, so a
sustained rise in crude prices widens the current account deficit and is usually Rupee-negative, which in turn
pressures import-heavy sectors and eases pressure on net exporters.

Minervini-style trend definition (simplified): a stock is in a technical "Stage 2" uptrend when price trades
above both its 10-week and 20-week (or the daily-equivalent 10/20-period) moving averages, with the shorter
average above the longer one — the reverse ordering flags a Stage 4 downtrend.

Geopolitical/event risk (sanctions, war, trade restrictions) tends to spike volatility and widen risk premia
across the board before resolving one way or the other — position sizing, not direction-calling, is the usual
risk-management response while the event is live.
